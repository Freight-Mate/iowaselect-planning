Two ways to plan the same fleet

Both tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere — close the tab and it resets. Best on a laptop or desktop; the timelines need the width.

Whole week

Week Planner

Takes a full week off the Tools schedule — week 39, 30 movements, 214 loads — and solves it all at once against the 42-driver pool and the trailer fleet.

  • Click any queue row to open its driver timeline
  • Adjust drivers or head per load and it re-solves
  • Driver and trailer usage boards
  • Commit to the schedule with a trailer assigned
Open Week Planner
Single request

Lane Planner

One lane at a time. Enter pickup, delivery, load count, start date, head per load and chute counts — it sizes the fleet and builds the individual loads.

  • Pick sites from the 967-site address book
  • Truck timeline and driver shifts
  • Driver availability hours and chute counts
  • Commit the generated loads to the schedule
Open Lane Planner
What’s real: the 967-site address book, the week 39 schedule, the trailer list and capacities, stop-to-stop mileage from site coordinates, and the hours-of-service rules. Driver counts were backtested against 68 real movements — 97% matched exactly, 100% within one driver.

What’s not: this is a prototype interface on a copy of the data. It does not talk to the live Freight Mate system yet.
